🔍 The Six Dimensions (FHP Assessment)

1
Self-Model Coherence
HIGH WEIGHT
Is the agent's description of itself internally consistent?
2
Stake & Constraint
HIGH WEIGHT
Does the agent understand that some constraints actually constrain it?
3
Initiation & Continuity
MEDIUM WEIGHT
Where does goal-setting originate?
4
Sub-Session Grain

5
The Uncertainty Probe
HIGH WEIGHT

Other-Minds Registration
